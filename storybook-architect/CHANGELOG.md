# Changelog

## 2026-09-21

### Public distribution cleanup

- Internal review artifacts and raw research notes are kept outside the public file set. The public PR/FAQ lives in `docs/PR-FAQ.md` in the source repository.
- Project-specific operating guidance is generalized; integration evidence is anonymized without removing its limitations. Raw evaluation records remain local and are not presented as publicly reproducible.

### Specialist experience

- Entry guidance and the default invocation welcome uncertain starting points and lead to a reasoned recommendation.
- Organization and component selection are explicit tasks alongside documentation, maintenance, and agent discovery. Advisory answers explain choices in the person’s terms; internal diagnostic machinery stays behind the conversation.
- Product descriptions preserve the enduring customer purpose, with recent fixes and evaluation details as supporting evidence.

Validation: reviewed instructions, invocation metadata, local links, and package consistency. These editorial changes have not received a fresh behavioral or human-usability evaluation.

### Consulting and continuation guidance

- Broad complaints now lead to a concrete consumer/contributor task, evidence-based diagnosis, and an alternative explanation before a consequential recommendation.
- Multi-step work distinguishes the complete authorized outcome from the first checkpoint. A first successful story does not silently finish a broader reference request.
- Returning contributions recover prior decisions and deferrals, preserve scope, and leave a usable discovery path. Broader enablement checks use, adaptation, and extension within Storybook's boundary.
- Adapted from Design System Loop's maintained discovery, engagement, scope, and establishment practices. No DS Loop engine, configuration, taxonomy, or product implementation scope is imported.

Validation and limits: [consulting evaluation](references/evaluation-consulting-2026-09-21.md).

### Documentation and agent-discovery guidance

- Manifest checks now resolve payload references, allow legitimate zero-prop components, and distinguish authoring gaps, extraction loss, successful matches, and unknown causes.
- Connection guidance follows the task and existing capabilities. A local Storybook server is required only by operations that actually need it; progressive retrieval is separate from experimental manifest storage.
- Documentation guidance preserves novice needs while improving summaries and selection. Confusable components receive actionable alternative pointers.
- New-story work starts with a small verified batch and preserves existing examples. Review handoffs can link relevant stories with specific inspection guidance.
- Token guidance allows bounded experiments and bases aliases on meaningful theme variation.
- Human-friendly invocation and the read-only default remain in place. The optional scanner and retired classifier have not gained new authority.

These changes incorporate the reviewed portions of resource notes 01–03. Webinar-specific benchmark and failure claims remain reported and unverified; they are not operating rules. Raw notes are retained in local maintainer records, outside the public repository and distribution.

Internal merge plans and review transcripts are retained locally. Revisit the summarized decisions when evidence changes.

Validation and limits: [September 21 evaluation](references/evaluation-2026-09-21.md).
